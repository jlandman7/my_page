About me

Hi! I'm Jacob. I'm currently a full time student at UC Berkeley studying mathematics, astrophysics, computer science, and music. I love beautiful mornings, fast-paced days, and sparkly silent evenings. I take inspiration from plants, space, water, and people who radiate kindness. I enjoy solving problems, collaborating on design decisions, and working hard to create cool things.

Mathematics

As a math student, I've learned first and foremost to be patient when problem solving. When I was taking my first analysis classes, I found a new depth of problem solving learning to sculpt convincing and formal arguments, unlocking new levels of assuredness and applicability. I believe the mathematical thinking process is a very useful tool for coming to decisions in the right contexts.

Computer Science

I've been coding since I was about 10 years old, starting with python and eventually moving toward Java, Javascript, and C++, among others. I find software development really fun, solving each little problem to build up to an awesome final product, and appreciate the massive power of computation these days. I have built a few apps on my own, largely for recreation, and have experience coding with and without AI.
